🚀 **Introducing CarePath: Smart Transportation Coordination for Rural Healthcare Access**

Every day, patients in rural and low-income communities miss critical medical appointments — dialysis, oncology, cardiology, post-surgical follow-ups — not by choice, but because they have no reliable way to get there.

We've built **CarePath** to solve this systemic challenge through intelligent transportation coordination that puts people first.

## 🎯 The Problem
- Medicaid transport cancellations with no notice
- Volunteer drivers with no coordination tool
- Manual phone/text management for coordinators
- **Zero fallback when rides fail** → no-shows, health deterioration, avoidable ER visits

## 💡 Our Solution
**Request → Match → Confirm → Complete → Survey**

With automatic fallback escalation the moment a ride is at risk.

## 👥 Who We Serve

**Patients:** Reliable rides, accessibility-first matching, mobile-first design

**Volunteer/NEMT Drivers:** Professional coordination tool, visibility into needs

**Coordinators:** One dashboard, live tracking, automatic fallback

**Institutional Partners:** Ride-credit funding, outcome tracking

**Caregivers/Advocates:** Arrange rides, real-time updates

## 🏆 What Makes Us Different
- Location-agnostic (mileage never a factor)
- Fallback & accessibility as core features
- Proven through 15 stakeholder interviews, 5 user types, 13 pain signals
- Focused on one coordination workflow end-to-end

## 📊 Impact
Fewer missed appointments → fewer ER visits → better patient outcomes.

**Scaling from Arkansas to communities nationwide where public transit is limited and Medicaid transport is unreliable.**
