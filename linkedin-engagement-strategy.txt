LinkedIn Engagement Strategy

## Content Types & Frequency

### Daily Micro-Content (3-5 posts/week)
- **Feature Highlights:** "How CarePath handles [specific feature]"
- **User Story:** "Meet Jane, a CarePath coordinator in [state]"
- **Technical Insight:** "Our approach to [technical challenge]"
- **Team Spotlights:** "Why our engineering team loves building CarePath"
- **Impact Updates:** "Month in Review: Lives impacted this month"

### Weekly Deep-Dive (1 post/week)
- Stakeholder group focus
- Technical architecture
- Product roadmap updates
- Community partnerships

### Bi-Weekly Comprehensive (1 post/2 weeks)
- Technical deep-dive
- Impact report
- Roadmap update
- Partnership announcements

## Engagement Tactics

### Comments & Discussion
- Ask questions about transportation challenges
- Request suggestions for feature improvements
- Share behind-the-scenes development stories
- Poll stakeholder groups on priorities
- Highlight real user success stories from across the country

### Visual Content
- Screenshots of the CarePath dashboard
- Infographics about impact metrics
- Photos of team events or hackathons
- Screenshots of mobile app interfaces
- Accessibility feature demonstrations

### Social Proof
- Quote from beta users
- Partnership announcements
- Press coverage
- Awards/recognitions
- Community testimonials

### Industry Connections
- Link to relevant research studies
- Reference complementary initiatives
- Share thought leadership articles
- Partner with healthcare technology organizations

## Best Practices

### Post Timing
- **Optimal:** 8-10 AM EST (morning commute)
- **Secondary:** 12 PM EST (lunch)
- **Avoid:** After-hours when LinkedIn is less active

### Post Length
- **Optimal:** 150-300 characters for initial hook
- **Full post:** 400-800 characters for detailed content
- **Include:** 3-5 relevant hashtags

### Call-to-Action
- Clear next step
- Specific link
- Professional tone
- Multiple engagement options

## Metrics to Track
