🚀 **Introducing CarePath: The Transportation Coordination Platform That's Transforming Rural Healthcare Access**

Every day, patients in rural and low-income communities miss critical medical appointments — dialysis, oncology, cardiology, post-surgical follow-ups — not by choice, but because they have no reliable way to get there.

We've built **CarePath** to solve this systemic challenge through smart transportation coordination that puts people first.

## 🎯 **The Problem**
- Medicaid transport cancellations with no notice
- Volunteer drivers with no coordination tool
- Manual phone/text management for coordinators
- **Zero fallback when rides fail** → leads to no-shows, health deterioration, avoidable ER visits

## 💡 **Our Solution: CarePath**
**From Request → Match → Confirm → Complete → Survey**

With automatic fallback escalation the moment a ride is at risk.

## 👥 **Who We Serve**

### **Patients**
✅ Reliable rides to essential medical appointments  
✅ Accessibility-first matching (wheelchair, oxygen, stretcher)  
✅ Mobile-first design for how patients actually access care

### **Volunteer/NEMT Drivers**  
✅ A real coordination tool instead of ad-hoc texts  
✅ Visibility into patient needs and ride status  
✅ Professional mobile app for coordination

### **Coordinators**
✅ One dashboard instead of manual phone/paper juggling  
✅ Live ride tracking and automatic fallback management  
✅ Full communication logs for every ride

### **Institutional Partners**
✅ Ride-credit funding system for community investment  
✅ Outcome tracking and impact measurement  
✅ Scalable transportation solution

### **Caregivers/Advocates**
✅ Arrange rides on behalf of patients  
✅ Real-time updates and communication  
✅ Accessibility support

## 🏆 **What Makes CarePath Different**
- **Location-agnostic** — never uses mileage as a decision factor
- **Fallback and accessibility are core features**, not afterthoughts
- **Proven through 15 stakeholder interviews, 5 user types, 13 pain signals validated**
- **Focused on one coordination workflow** end-to-end, not a broad marketplace

## 📊 **The Impact**
Fewer missed appointments → fewer avoidable ER visits → better outcomes for patients managing dialysis, oncology, and cardiology care.

**Scaling from Arkansas to communities nationwide where public transit is limited and Medicaid transportation is unreliable.**
